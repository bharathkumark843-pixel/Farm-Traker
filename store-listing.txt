FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max):
Farm Tracker is a simple, senior-friendly pesticide / insecticide tracker made
for farmers. Big buttons, minimal typing, English + Telugu.

- Track your pesticides, insecticides, herbicides and fungicides
- Scan barcodes/QR codes instead of typing
- Log every spray: crop, field, quantity — stock updates automatically
- Low-stock, expiring-soon and expired alerts
- Reports with CSV + PDF export
- Government schemes & farm loans info (English + Telugu)
- 100% OFFLINE — works with no internet; your data stays on your phone

No dosage advice: the app stores the official label text from the bottle and
your own records only.
