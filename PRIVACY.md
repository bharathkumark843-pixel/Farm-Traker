# Farm Tracker — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 2026

Farm Tracker is an offline-first pesticide/insecticide record-keeping app for farmers.

## Data we collect: none

- We operate **no servers** and run **no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs**.
- All data you enter (products, usage logs, profile details such as your mobile
  number, village, and land size) is stored **only on your own phone**, in the
  app's private on-device database.
- Nothing you enter is transmitted anywhere by default.

## Optional cloud backup

- If cloud backup is enabled on your copy of the app, your product and usage
  records are copied to a private database (Supabase) namespaced to your mobile
  number, so they survive phone loss. This happens only when you have internet
  and only for your own records. You can use the app fully without it.
- When cloud backup is off (the default), the app works 100% offline and sends
  no data at all.

## Camera and photos

- The camera is used only when you tap Scan or Take Photo: to read a product
  barcode/QR code and to attach a bottle photo to your own record.
- Photos are stored on your phone as part of your records (and in your cloud
  backup, if enabled). They are never shared with anyone.

## Deletion

- Deleting a product or log inside the app removes it from your phone.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all on-device data permanently.
- To request deletion of a cloud backup copy, contact the app distributor
  (your details are in the Play Store listing) with your registered mobile number.

## Children

- The app is a general farm utility, not directed at children, and collects no
  data from anyone.

## Changes

- If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page
  and the app's store listing will be updated.
